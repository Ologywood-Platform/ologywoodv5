# Simple Ryder Template Guide

## Overview

The Simple Ryder Template is a clean, user-friendly tool for artists to create and manage their performance requirements. Unlike complex rider documents, this template focuses on the essential information venues need to know to host a successful performance.

**Key Features:**
- 4-tab interface for easy navigation
- Clear, descriptive labels and helpful hints
- Live preview before saving
- Conditional fields that appear only when relevant
- Mobile-friendly design

---

## What is a Ryder?

A **ryder** (or rider) is a document that outlines an artist's performance requirements and preferences. It communicates to venues:
- What equipment and technical setup you need
- Your comfort and hospitality requirements
- Your cancellation policy and emergency contact
- Any special requests or preferences

Think of it as a professional "rider" that travels with your booking contract.

---

## Template Sections

### 1. Basic Information Tab

This section captures the fundamental details about your performance.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Template Name** | Yes | A memorable name for this template (e.g., "Standard Concert Setup", "Festival Rig") |
| **Performance Type** | Yes | The type of performance: Concert, DJ Set, Acoustic, Workshop, or Other |
| **Number of Performers** | No | How many people will be performing (band members, crew, etc.) |
| **Performance Duration** | Yes | How long the performance will last in minutes (e.g., 60, 90, 120) |
| **Setup Time** | No | Minutes needed before the performance starts for setup and soundcheck |

**Tips:**
- Use descriptive template names so you can quickly find the right one for each booking
- If you perform different types of shows, create separate templates for each
- Setup time typically includes soundcheck, line check, and final preparations

---

### 2. Technical Requirements Tab

This section covers all the technical equipment and setup your performance needs.

#### Essential Technical Needs

| Field | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **PA System Required** | Checkbox | Does the venue need to provide sound amplification? |
| **Lighting Required** | Checkbox | Do you need special stage lighting? |
| **Monitor Mix Required** | Checkbox | Do you need stage monitors to hear yourself? |
| **Bringing Own Equipment** | Checkbox | Will you bring your own gear? |

#### Conditional Fields (appear when relevant)

| Field | Appears When | Description |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| **Equipment List** | "Bringing Own Equipment" is checked | Detailed list of what you're bringing (drums, amplifier, cables, etc.) |
| **Power Requirements** | Always available | Any special power needs (e.g., "2 x 20A circuits", "110V outlet") |

**Tips:**
- Be specific about equipment you're bringing - venues need to plan for load-in and power
- If you don't have special power needs, leave this blank
- Most venues can provide basic PA and lighting - only check these if you have specific requirements

---

### 3. Hospitality Tab

This section covers your comfort and well-being needs during the event.

| Field | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Dressing Room Required** | Checkbox | Do you need a private space to prepare before the show? |
| **Parking Required** | Checkbox | Do you need parking for your vehicle/equipment? |
| **Catering Provided** | Checkbox | Should the venue provide food and drinks? |

#### Conditional Fields

| Field | Appears When | Description |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| **Dietary Restrictions** | "Catering Provided" is checked | Any allergies or dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan, nut allergy, gluten-free, etc.) |

**Tips:**
- Most venues appreciate knowing about dietary needs in advance
- Be specific about allergies for safety reasons
- If you don't need a dressing room, venues can save space and resources

---

### 4. Special Requests Tab

This section captures any additional information venues should know.

| Field | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Special Requests** | Text Area | Any additional requirements or preferences |
| **Cancellation Policy** | Text Area | Your cancellation terms (e.g., "50% deposit non-refundable if cancelled within 7 days") |
| **Emergency Contact** | Phone | Contact number for day-of emergencies |

**Tips:**
- Use Special Requests for things like: "Need a specific microphone type", "Prefer morning soundcheck", "Need WiFi access"
- Clearly state your cancellation policy to avoid misunderstandings
- Always provide an emergency contact for the day of the event

## How to Use the Template

### Creating a New Template

1. **Go to your Artist Dashboard**
   - Click "Dashboard" in the navigation
   - Click "Riders" in the left sidebar
   - Click "Create New Template"

2. **Fill in Basic Information**
   - Give your template a memorable name
   - Select your performance type
   - Enter how long your performance typically lasts
   - Specify setup time needed

3. **Add Technical Requirements**
   - Check the boxes for equipment/setup you need
   - If bringing your own equipment, list what you're bringing
   - Specify any power requirements

4. **Set Hospitality Needs**
   - Check boxes for dressing room, parking, catering
   - If catering is needed, specify dietary restrictions

5. **Add Special Requests**
   - Include any additional requirements
   - State your cancellation policy
   - Provide an emergency contact number

6. **Preview Your Template**
   - Click "Preview" to see how venues will see your ryder
   - Review all information for accuracy
   - Click "Edit" to make changes if needed

7. **Save Your Template**
   - Click "Save Template"
   - Your template is now ready to use for bookings

### Using Templates for Bookings

When you receive a booking request:

1. **Select Your Template**
   - Choose the template that best matches the booking
   - Most bookings will use your "Standard" template

2. **Customize for the Venue** (if needed)
   - Some venues may have different requirements
   - You can create a new template or modify an existing one

3. **Send to Venue**
   - The venue will see your ryder when reviewing your booking request
   - They can acknowledge receipt and confirm they can meet your requirements

4. **Track Acknowledgments**
   - Your dashboard shows which venues have acknowledged your ryder
   - Follow up if a venue hasn't confirmed within 2 weeks of booking

### Editing an Existing Template

1. Go to Dashboard → Riders
2. Click the template you want to edit
3. Click "Edit"
4. Make your changes
5. Click "Save Template"
6. Your template version will be updated

### Deleting a Template

1. Go to Dashboard → Riders
2. Find the template you want to delete
3. Click the delete icon (trash can)
4. Confirm deletion

## Best Practices

### Keep It Professional
- Use clear, professional language
- Avoid jargon or overly casual tone
- Spell-check before saving

### Be Specific
- Instead of "good PA", say "PA with subwoofers"
- Instead of "catering", specify "vegetarian meals for 5 people"
- Instead of "special setup", list exactly what you need

### Be Realistic
- Only request what you truly need
- Venues are more likely to book if your requirements are reasonable
- Excessive requirements may scare away smaller venues

### Update Regularly
- Review your templates every 6 months
- Update if your equipment or needs change
- Create new templates for new performance types

### Create Multiple Templates
- **Standard Concert** - Your typical full band setup
- **Acoustic** - Minimal setup for intimate venues
- **Festival** - Outdoor setup with specific requirements
- **Workshop** - Educational performance with different needs

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use the same template for all my bookings?**
A: Yes! If your requirements are consistent, one template works for most bookings. You can create additional templates for different performance types.

**Q: What if a venue can't meet my requirements?**
A: Communicate directly with the venue. They may be able to accommodate some requests or suggest alternatives. Your emergency contact is for these conversations.

**Q: How often should I update my template?**
A: Review it every 6 months or whenever your equipment/needs change. Keep it current so venues have accurate information.

**Q: Can venues modify my ryder?**
A: Venues can acknowledge your ryder and note any limitations, but they can't change your template. You control what's in your ryder.

**Q: Should I include pricing in my ryder?**
A: No. Pricing is handled separately in the booking agreement. Your ryder focuses on technical and hospitality needs.

**Q: What if I need to make changes after sending a ryder?**
A: Contact the venue directly. You can also update your template for future bookings.
