## NOTES FOR ARTISTS USING THIS TEMPLATE

1. **Customize for Your Needs:** Modify this template to reflect your specific requirements. Not all sections may apply to your performance style.

2. **Be Realistic:** While it's good to have standards, be flexible on non-essential items to increase booking opportunities.

3. **Prioritize:** Clearly mark which items are "must-haves" vs. "nice-to-haves" to help venues understand your priorities.

4. **Communication:** Use this rider as a conversation starter, not a rigid contract. Discuss any concerns with the venue before the event.

5. **Professional Tone:** Keep language professional and courteous. Remember, venues want to work with easy-to-work-with artists.

6. **Update Regularly:** Review and update your rider as your needs change or as you gain experience with different venue types.

---

## NOTES FOR VENUES USING THIS TEMPLATE

1. **Negotiate in Good Faith:** Use this rider as a starting point for discussion, not a final demand.

2. **Ask Questions:** If anything is unclear, contact the artist or their representative immediately.

3. **Plan Ahead:** Address rider requirements well in advance of the performance date.

4. **Document Everything:** Keep a copy of the signed/agreed-upon rider for your records.

5. **Communicate Changes:** If you cannot meet a requirement, discuss alternatives with the artist as soon as possible.

6. **Day-of Coordination:** Assign a venue contact person to coordinate with the artist on the day of performance.

## SAMPLE COMPLETED RIDER (Example)

### Artist Information
- **Artist Name:** Luna Echo
- **Genre:** Indie Pop, Electronic
- **Performance Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Setup Time:** 30 minutes
- **Fee:** $2,500 (50% deposit due upon confirmation, 50% due day of performance)

### Technical Requirements
- PA System: Yes (minimum 2000W)
- Microphones: 2 vocal mics with stands
- Monitor Mix: 1 monitor mix
- Soundcheck: 30 minutes (preferred 2 hours before performance)
- Lighting: Basic stage lighting with color capability
- Electrical: 2 dedicated 20A circuits

### Hospitality
- Dressing Room: Yes, with mirror and seating
- Meals: 1 meal for 2 people (vegetarian options)
- Beverages: Water, juice, coffee
- Parking: 2 spaces for vehicles

### Personnel
- Band Members: 2 (vocalist + keyboardist)
- Crew: 1 sound technician
- Total: 3 people

### Special Notes
- Artist will provide backing tracks via USB
- Venue must provide professional sound engineer
- No flash photography during performance
- Social media promotion welcome with artist credit
