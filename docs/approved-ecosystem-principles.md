# Approved OlogyWood Ecosystem Principles

Source: user-provided `pasted_content.txt`, received September 2, 2026.

## Strategic center

OlogyWood is the creator economy operating system for talent, venues, fans, brands, and supporters. It combines identity, discovery, business operations, relationships, commerce, events, media, memberships, and audience ownership. The platform should behave as a connected ecosystem, not a collection of disconnected tools.

## Product flywheel

The approved ecosystem loop is:

> **Discover → Connect → Experience → Support → Return**

Discovery introduces people, venues, events, and live or hosted content. The public creator or venue profile is the center of the ecosystem. From that profile, visitors must immediately understand what they can do: **Book, Attend, Buy, Watch/Listen, Follow, or Join**. Transactions should lead to retained relationships, and My Ology should bring supporters back through tickets, purchases, bookings, follows, memberships, and accessible content.

## Navigation principles

Discover is the primary public entry point. Experiences, Shop, and Community are the other main public content destinations. My Ology is the supporter’s return destination. Workspace is the role-aware business destination. Search, Create, Inbox, Notifications, and Account operate as global utilities rather than competing product pillars. Supporting pages such as Blog, Pricing, Help, Sponsors, and company information belong in a secondary More/Learn area.

## Role principles

One account may participate as a creator, venue operator, fan, team member, or administrator. The global shell should remain consistent while role-specific controls and language adapt. Administrator complexity must stay separate from creator, venue, fan, and public experiences. Creator-owners with admin privileges should default to their creator identity and explicitly switch into Admin Workspace when needed.

## Technical boundaries

User-facing status language should be shared—Draft, Live, Pending, Confirmed/Paid, In progress, Fulfilled/Completed, and Cancelled/Refunded—but bookings, ticket orders, merchandise/book orders, subscriptions, releases, and live sessions should remain specialized technical domains. The goal is one understandable lifecycle in the interface, not one overloaded database object.

## Implementation priority

The approved first ecosystem phase is the Unified Workspace with Needs Attention and role-aware + Create actions, the centralized My Ology fan home, the six-destination global navigation, profile-centered action rails, and compatibility aliases for existing feature URLs. Existing transaction engines and the current Content Release model remain intact during this phase.
