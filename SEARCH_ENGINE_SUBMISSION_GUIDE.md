# Search Engine Submission Guide for Ologywood

This guide walks you through submitting your sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to make your platform discoverable in search results.

---

## Part 1: Submit to Google Search Console

### Step 1: Access Google Search Console
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Sign in with your Google account (create one if needed)
3. Click **"Add property"** or **"Start now"**

### Step 2: Add Your Domain
1. Choose **"URL prefix"** option
2. Enter your domain: `https://ologywood.manus.space` (or your custom domain if you have one)
3. Click **"Continue"**

### Step 3: Verify Ownership
Google will ask you to verify ownership. Choose one of these methods:

**Option A: HTML file upload (Easiest)**
1. Download the HTML verification file
2. Upload it to your website's public folder
3. Click "Verify"

**Option B: Meta tag (Recommended for Manus)**
1. Copy the meta tag provided
2. Add it to your homepage `<head>` section in Home.tsx
3. Click "Verify"

**Option C: Google Analytics**
1. If you have Google Analytics connected, click "Verify"

### Step 4: Submit Your Sitemap
1. Once verified, go to **"Sitemaps"** in the left menu
2. Click **"Add/test sitemaps"**
3. Enter: `https://ologywood.manus.space/sitemap.xml`
4. Click **"Submit"**

### Step 5: Monitor Performance
- Check **"Performance"** tab to see search impressions and clicks
- Review **"Coverage"** to see which pages are indexed
- Check **"Mobile Usability"** for mobile optimization issues

## Part 2: Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools

### Step 1: Access Bing Webmaster Tools
1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)
2. Sign in with your Microsoft account (create one if needed)
3. Click **"Add a site"**

### Step 2: Add Your Domain
1. Enter your domain: `https://ologywood.manus.space`
2. Click **"Add"**

### Step 3: Verify Ownership
Bing offers multiple verification methods:

**Option A: XML file (Easiest)**
1. Download the XML verification file
2. Upload to your public folder
3. Click "Verify"

**Option B: Meta tag**
1. Copy the meta tag
2. Add to your homepage `<head>` section
3. Click "Verify"

**Option C: CNAME record (Advanced)**
1. Add a CNAME record to your domain DNS
2. Click "Verify"

### Step 4: Submit Your Sitemap
1. Go to **"Sitemaps"** in the left menu
2. Click **"Submit sitemap"**
3. Enter: `https://ologywood.manus.space/sitemap.xml`
4. Click **"Submit"**

### Step 5: Monitor Performance
- Check **"Search traffic"** to see impressions and clicks
- Review **"Crawl information"** to see indexing status
- Check **"Diagnostics"** for any crawling issues

## Part 3: What Happens Next?

### Immediate (1-7 days)
- Search engines crawl your sitemap
- Pages start appearing in search index
- You may see first impressions in Search Console

### Short-term (1-4 weeks)
- More pages get indexed
- You start seeing search traffic
- Rankings begin to stabilize

### Long-term (1-3 months)
- Organic search traffic increases
- Rankings improve with backlinks and content
- You can see trending keywords and opportunities

## Part 5: Troubleshooting

### Sitemap Not Submitting?
- Verify your domain is correctly verified
- Check that sitemap.xml is publicly accessible at `https://ologywood.manus.space/sitemap.xml`
- Ensure robots.txt allows crawling of sitemap.xml

### Pages Not Indexed?
- Check "Coverage" report in Search Console
- Look for crawl errors or blocked resources
- Ensure pages are not blocked by robots.txt
- Check for noindex meta tags

### No Search Traffic After 2 Weeks?
- This is normal! Indexing takes time
- Focus on content quality and backlinks
- Monitor Search Console for impressions
