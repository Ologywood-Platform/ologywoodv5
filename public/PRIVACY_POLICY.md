# Privacy Policy

**Ologywood**  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Ologywood ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Ologywood platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration:** When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and password.

**Profile Information:** Artists and venues provide additional information including bio, location, photos, payment information, and business details.

**Payment Information:** We collect payment details through Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers directly—Stripe handles all payment processing.

**Communication:** When you contact us, send messages through the platform, or participate in support, we collect the content of those communications.

**Verification Documents:** For identity verification, we collect government-issued ID, passport, or driver's license information.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:** We collect device type, operating system, browser type, and unique device identifiers.

**Usage Information:** We track pages visited, features used, booking history, and time spent on the platform.

**Location Information:** We collect approximate location based on IP address (not precise GPS).

**Cookies and Similar Technologies:** We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

**Payment Processor:** Stripe provides payment confirmation and transaction details.

**Analytics Providers:** Third-party analytics services provide usage insights.

**Social Media:** If you link social media accounts, we collect publicly available profile information.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

**Service Delivery:** Providing booking services, processing payments, and managing your account.

**Communication:** Sending transactional emails, support responses, and platform notifications.

**Verification:** Verifying your identity, preventing fraud, and ensuring platform safety.

**Analytics:** Understanding how users interact with our platform to improve services.

**Legal Compliance:** Meeting tax reporting requirements, responding to legal requests, and complying with regulations.

**Marketing:** Sending promotional emails (with your consent) and displaying targeted advertisements.

**Security:** Detecting and preventing fraudulent activity, unauthorized access, and security threats.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, we process your information based on:

**Consent:** You have explicitly agreed to our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

**Contract:** Processing is necessary to provide the booking services you requested.

**Legal Obligation:** We must comply with tax reporting, financial regulations, and law enforcement requests.

**Legitimate Interest:** We have a legitimate interest in platform safety, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 Information We Share

**Service Providers:** We share information with third parties who assist in operating our platform (Stripe, SendGrid, AWS, analytics providers).

**Artists and Venues:** Booking information is shared between artists and venues as necessary to complete transactions.

**Legal Requirements:** We disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request.

**Business Transfers:** If Ologywood is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

**Aggregated Data:** We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytics and research.

### 5.2 Information We Do NOT Share

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We do NOT share your credit card information—Stripe handles all payment data.

## 6. Data Retention

**Account Information:** Retained while your account is active and for 3 years after deletion.

**Payment Records:** Retained for 7 years (required for tax reporting).

**Messages and Communications:** Retained for 1 year after deletion.

**Identity Verification Documents:** Retained for 90 days after verification (or until dispute resolution).

**Analytics Data:** Retained for 1 year.

**Audit Logs:** Retained for 2 years.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

**Right to Access:** You can request a copy of your personal data.

**Right to Deletion:** You can request deletion of your account and personal data (with exceptions for tax records).

**Right to Rectification:** You can update or correct your information.

**Right to Restrict Processing:** You can restrict how we use your data.

**Right to Data Portability:** You can request your data in a portable format.

**Right to Object:** You can object to certain types of processing (marketing, analytics).

**Right to Lodge a Complaint:** You can file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

### 7.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

**Right to Know:** You can request what personal information we collect.

**Right to Delete:** You can request deletion of your personal information.

**Right to Opt-Out:** You can opt-out of data sales (though we don't sell data).

**Right to Non-Discrimination:** We don't discriminate against users who exercise their rights.

### 7.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@ologywood.com** with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

**Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using SSL/TLS. Sensitive data at rest is encrypted.

**Access Controls:** Only authorized employees with a legitimate need can access personal data.

**Authentication:** We use strong password requirements and multi-factor authentication for admin accounts.

**Regular Audits:** We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing.

**Incident Response:** We have a documented incident response plan and notify affected users of data breaches within 72 hours (as required by law).

## 9. Cookies and Tracking

### 9.1 Types of Cookies We Use

**Essential Cookies:** Required for platform functionality (authentication, security).

**Performance Cookies:** Help us understand how users interact with the platform.

**Marketing Cookies:** Used to display targeted advertisements (only with consent).

### 9.2 Cookie Management

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may limit platform functionality.

### 9.3 Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. We respect this signal and do not track users who enable it.

## 10. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing information.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

## 12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws different from your home country. By using our Service, you consent to such transfers.

## 13. California Privacy Rights

**California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):** California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. See Section 7.2 above for details.

**California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA):** We do not track users across third-party websites. We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, but we respect browser-level Do Not Track settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting the updated policy on our website. Your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
