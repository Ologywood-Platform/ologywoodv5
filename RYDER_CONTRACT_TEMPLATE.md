## 12. TEMPLATE USAGE GUIDE

### For Artists:
1. **Customize your rider** - Fill in sections that apply to your performance type
2. **Be specific** - Provide exact equipment models and quantities when possible
3. **Keep it reasonable** - Request what you actually need for a quality performance
4. **Update regularly** - Modify your rider as your needs change
5. **Communicate clearly** - Use this as a starting point for venue discussions

### For Venues:
1. **Review carefully** - Understand all requirements before confirming the booking
2. **Clarify uncertainties** - Contact the artist if any requirements are unclear
3. **Plan ahead** - Ensure you can meet requirements with adequate lead time
4. **Confirm details** - Verify all arrangements 1 week before the performance
5. **Document agreements** - Keep signed copies for your records

## 13. COMMON RIDER VARIATIONS

### Solo Acoustic Performance
- Minimal technical requirements
- Single microphone and stand
- Basic PA system
- No lighting rig required
- Portable equipment only

### DJ Performance
- DJ booth setup (6ft x 3ft minimum)
- Turntables or CDJ equipment
- Mixer and headphones
- Lighting system for dance floor
- Dance floor space (minimum 20ft x 20ft)

### Full Band Performance
- Complete PA system
- Multiple microphones and stands
- Monitor system with separate mixes
- Professional lighting rig
- Large stage area (minimum 20ft x 15ft)
- Dressing rooms for multiple performers

### Acoustic Duo/Trio
- 2-3 microphones
- Basic PA system
- Minimal lighting
- Small stage area (minimum 12ft x 10ft)
- Single dressing room

## 15. BEST PRACTICES

### For Artists Creating Riders:
✅ **DO:**
- Be clear and specific about requirements
- Provide alternative options when possible
- Include contact information for technical support
- Update rider regularly as needs change
- Be professional and courteous in tone

❌ **DON'T:**
- Make unreasonable demands
- Include vague or ambiguous requirements
- Demand items that aren't essential
- Change requirements at the last minute
- Ignore venue limitations or constraints

### For Venues Reviewing Riders:
✅ **DO:**
- Read the entire rider carefully
- Ask clarifying questions early
- Plan ahead to meet requirements
- Confirm arrangements in writing
- Provide feedback on any issues

❌ **DON'T:**
- Ignore technical requirements
- Promise things you can't deliver
- Make last-minute changes
- Assume the artist will be flexible
- Skip the sound check
